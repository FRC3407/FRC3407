### Kickoff weekend

Kickoff weekend is when all the FRC teams in the world learn what the game is going to be for the year. Our teams spends two days reading the rules, coming up with a strategy, and brainstorming functionality for our robot. It is highly recommended that all team members attend, as kickoff is one of the most impactful events of the season.



### Week Zero Competition

Week zero is our first competition of the season. It is a great chance to test our robot in a real game environment and see what other people are doing without the high stakes. This is an incredible opportunity to learn how to improve our robot for the regional and post season compititions.



### 10,000 Lakes Regional Competition

This is the main competition of our season. It lasts several days and spans over a couple days of school.
