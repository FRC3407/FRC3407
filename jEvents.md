### Kickoff weekend

Kickoff weekend is when all the FRC teams learn what the game is going to be for the year. Our teams spends two days reading the rules, coming up with a strategy, brainstorming functionality for our robot, and having a good time. It is highly recommended that all team members come because what happens at kickoff impacts our entire season.

January 10 & 11 at Bethel University, 3900 Bethel Dr, St Paul


### Week Zero Competition

Week Zero is our first competition of the season. It's a great chance to test our robot in a competitive game environment and see what other people are doing without the high stakes. It is an incredible opportunity to learn how to improve our robot for the regional and post season competitions.

February 26 at Irondale High School, 2425 Long Lake Rd, New Brighton


### 10,000 Lakes Regional Competition

This is the main competition of our season. It lasts several days and spans over a couple days of school. 

March 25-28 at 3M Arena at Mariucci, 1901 4th St SE, Minneapolis
